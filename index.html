Hello!

I'm a public health professional based in Vancouver, British Columbia, working in cancer prevention and screening at a provincial cancer agency. I specialize in evidence synthesis and implementation science - translating complex health information into clear, actionable materials and programs for diverse communities.

My experience spans the full spectrum of health strategy: from identifying system barriers and building stakeholder partnerships, to launching provincial initiatives in collaboration with the Ministry of Health, creative agencies, social services and non-profits. I have deep knowledge in scaling outreach pilots for community engagement.

I'm motivated by work that bridges research-driven thinking and practical communication to ultimately decrease barriers to healthcare access for those who need it most.
