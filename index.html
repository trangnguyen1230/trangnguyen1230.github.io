Hello!

I'm a public health professional based in Vancouver, British Columbia, working in cancer prevention and screening at BC Cancer. I specialize in translating complex health information into clear, actionable materials and programs for diverse communities, combining research-driven thinking with practical communication and outreach experience.

My work includes developing knowledge translation resources for patients, providers, planners and the public, and supporting major program launches in partnership with the Ministry of Health, creative studios, marketing agencies, non-profits and social services.

I'm motivated by work that bridges evidence and equity, and I'm passionate about community engagement, having led hundreds of community screening workshops in BC.
