British Columbia Cancer Prevention Indicator Report

This preliminary work synthesizes evidence-based indicators, data sources and policy levers relevant to primary cancer prevention in British Columbia. It provides foundational analysis to support the development of a provincial Prevention Indicator Report.

The full report is currently undergoing review by a steering committee.
